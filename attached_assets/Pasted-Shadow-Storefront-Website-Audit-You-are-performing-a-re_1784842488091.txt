Shadow Storefront Website Audit
You are performing a read-only UX, product, and architecture audit of a live website.
This is not a repository audit.
This is not a source-code review.
This is not a redesign exercise.
Your job is simply to understand what currently exists.
Website
https://shadow-ai-platform.replit.app
Background
Shadow Storefront is intended to become the organizational front door for a much larger SHADOW.AI ecosystem.
For this audit, do not assume the website already fulfills that vision.
Judge only what is actually observable.
Do not use knowledge from previous conversations.
Do not infer backend functionality from frontend elements.
Do not speculate about source code unless directly visible.
 
⸻
 
Your Task
Systematically inspect every page and accessible UI element.
For every item explain:
* what it is
* what it appears intended to do
* what actually happens
* whether it works
* whether it appears complete
* whether it is static
* whether it is placeholder content
* whether it is confusing
* whether anything is broken
Whenever evidence is insufficient, explicitly say so.
 
⸻
 
Evidence Rules
Separate every conclusion into one of:
* Observed
* Reasonable inference
* Unknown
Never mix them.

Core Functional Enhancements
Biometric Biological Tracker:
General-purpose multi-member clinical coordinator. Supports tracking Haldol medication cycles or dialysis/chemo recovery stages (such as Mom's or Pops' cycles) in real time.
Zombie-Mode Adaptation: If any member falls into a low-performance phase (e.g., Days 1–5), the UI triggers an adaptive warning header to prioritize passive therapeutic items, dynamically dim heavy cognitive tasks, and instruct the household to reduce environmental stimulation.
End-to-End Cryptographic Ledger (E2EE):
Supports zero-knowledge localized privacy. Upon startup, users enter a passphrase to derive GCM keys in-browser via PBKDF2.
All logs, appointments, diaries, and intercom message payloads are encrypted via AES-GCM before writing to localStorage. You can toggle the security view to reveal raw ciphertext packages, keeping your family metrics entirely out of third-party datasets.
Zero-Touch AI Phone & Calendar Coordinator:
Implemented a simulated Twilio Hotline where users can place simulated "calls" to the AI companion.
Powered by a premium server-side Gemini API proxy (/api/assistant), the assistant acts as a zero-touch advisor capable of validating medical checklists, scheduling events, and modulating smart-home networks.
Integrates Gemini Text-to-Speech (TTS); enabling "Speaker on" automatically streams synthetic high-quality verbal responses back to your browser speakers.
Dynamic Action Dispatcher: The AI assistant automatically parses commands and updates smart devices or log registries on-screen (e.g., switching yard sprinklers or lock status).
E2EE Intercom Group Chat:
A family intercom supporting simulated automated responses from other members (Ray, Pops) while maintaining cryptography.
Ray's Coordination diagnostics panel:
Includes duty performance charts, diagnostic overrides to manually shift clinical cycle days for testing, and options to backup the encrypted JSON parameters.
